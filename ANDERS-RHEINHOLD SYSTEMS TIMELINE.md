## Why the paper-trail really dies when Dr. Anders closes the ledger

| Link in the chain         | How Dr. Anders makes SPEAR vanish                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Shell Company**         | Anders-Rheinhold Systems (A.R.S.) is the *prime* contractor of record. Dr. Anders chairs the Risk & Compliance sub-board and is therefore **the highest civilian signatory** on every Black-Project line-item tied to “Project SPEAR.”   The UC agreed to finance the project through proxy. No one except the Auditor ever had direct access to ensure maximum plausible deniability. The Auditor again, worked as a consultant for the UC. When the project was dismantled, his final report was enough to not investigate any further. |
| **Treaty**                | Within two weeks of the Armistice, every Directorate in the UC is drowning in *thousands* of decommission actions.  If a contractor submits the right closure packet— casualty list, scrap certificate, toxic-site hazard flag— **no one has resources to re-inspect** tiny depots.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Closure Packet**        | Anders files **Form UCDC-404-Q (“Category-Bravo Catastrophic Loss, Bio/Chem hazard”)** plus a *Level-Red insurance claim* with Axion Underwriters.  That automatically triggers the **QSIG-NULL tag** in the UC logistics database: • marks the site non-recoverable • archives all requisition numbers • cancels recurring lease/comms fees. Officially the site was lost. Unofficially the project was dismantled succesfully.                                                                                                          |
| **Auditor**               | Auditor 37-B—the only UC official who could contest the closure—died in the shoot-out.  His body lies in a scrubbed depot and his encrypted omnicomm never broadcast a counter-report.  Headquarters received the filing “Initial Decomm WIP” before disappearing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Plausible deniability** | Anders’ packet states that on-site coolant contamination makes salvage “cost-negative; high biohazard.”  Budget controllers prefer to bury the line-item rather than fund a hazardous-materials task force in an outer system.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Result**                | **Carver Hold becomes a ghost entry**: flagged as cleaned up, toxic, and financially settled.  No active watch-list, no follow-up inspection, no public records request will find it because the controlling contractor self-certified destruction and the UC signed off during post-war triage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

*Take-home*: Anders wasn’t the project’s inventor but **she is the keystone accountant**.  Her signature plus the war-time paperwork avalanche is enough to bury the site forever.

## 2  How the master-slate escapes the station

### Canon hook: “only 12 SRC slates in existence”

*Carver Hold was issued ********************SRC 7 / 12********************.  UC policy required that the slate ********************never be left on an unmanned site********************—if the base is evacuated, the slate goes with the senior UC authority.*

### Narrative chain

1. **Pre-evac grab**
   *Auditor 37-B* (wounded) follows protocol: pockets **SRC 7/12** so no outsider can unlock SPEAR later.
2. **Escape-pod wreck**
   Auditor, a marine escort, and one loader operator board a three-seat pod.  Petrović’s lockdown corrupted nav tables; the craft drifts into the Piazzi debris belt and breaks up.
3. **Years of drift → salvage**
   In 2321 a freelance Aranae-class junk hauler (*SS Haywire Kate*) scoops the cracked pod.  The hauler’s owner fences anything not nailed down at **The Red Mile** (Porrima III).
4. **Underworld resale**
   A Red Mile fixer can’t decrypt the slate but recognises “mil-grade root cert” vibes; trades it to a Neon tech-runner for Joyeuse dust.  Word of the device circulates for years in hacker circles as the **“UC ghost key that fits nothing.”**
5. **Player acquisition (your entry point)**
   *Option A – vendor find*: the Neon runner dies owing debts; player buys a lock-box from a Dino Taviar pawn stall.
   *Option B – quest reward*: Ryujin side-contract has the player steal an “old UC smart-card” from a rival, not realising its magnitude.
   *Option C – random salvage*: procedural derelict in the Piazzi belt contains the corpse of the marine still clutching burnt slate.

Now the slate is in the player’s hands **without ever sitting inside CARVER HOLD** and without implicating Dr Anders directly.

### Quick answers to your two bullets

* **Was Dr Anders the driving force?**
  *Politically yes, technically no.*  She is the *financial* architect who signed the cut-out contracts and later filed the burial paperwork.  Her motive: protect A.R.S.’s reputation and keep the option of reviving the tech later through “secondary holdings” (Site MANTA).

* **Why isn’t the slate in the facility?**
  UC protocol + Auditor’s grab + escape-pod loss neatly extracts **SRC 7/12** from the station long before it becomes a tomb, letting it pass through the underworld to your player.

These explanations close the logical gaps, keep the secrecy airtight, and still give the campaign a clean way to place the master-slate wherever you want the player to find it.
